Three days that make the case

    3.05 miles at a 10:25 pace. Slow. Genuinely unimpressive. It matters only because it’s the first line of a log that hasn’t stopped since.

What that actually adds up to

  • I show up on the days it isn’t fun. Statistically, that’s most days.
  • I keep the promises I make when no one is checking. I’ve found that generalizes.
  • I built the dashboard this data lives in, by hand, for an audience of one. I like making people things. Given time, that would probably be you.

The rest of it — what I do all day, where I grew up, whether I’m any good in a kitchen — I’d genuinely rather tell you in person than put on a webpage.

So — hi.
